# Iterative experience updates

At a project milestone or when a meaningful correction occurs:

1. Save the relevant user-visible source, tool output, or artifact location; identify message/line and version. Do not reconstruct missing history.
2. Record the initial claim, action, failure or correction, evidence, outcome, and remaining uncertainty. Separate observed facts from inference.
3. Add a candidate entry with unique ID, source locator, consequence, correction, applicability, verification status, and revision date.

When a source archive is partial, report the exact reviewed scope and list missing branches, attachments, or outputs as gaps; never claim whole-account or whole-project coverage without evidence. Treat a gap as a limit on claims that depend on it, not an automatic blocker for every deliverable. Continue with a bounded report or skill version when the available evidence supports it; pause only a decision or claim that the missing material could change.

For long source reviews, update coverage immediately after each complete segment. At each five-segment or source boundary, reconcile unique IDs and missing checkpoints, compare all progress summaries to the index, record the next exact source locator, and preserve older snapshots as dated history.
4. Preserve prior entry text and source material. Add a changelog entry explaining the evidence and changed behavior.
5. Keep unverified or conflicting observations at case level. Promote them to general guidance only after independent support or a clear invariant is established.
6. Run the relevant case replay and utility checks after changing behavior. If checks fail, keep the prior official skill and record the pending change.
